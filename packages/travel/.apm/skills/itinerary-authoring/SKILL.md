# Itinerary Authoring

A good itinerary is not a list of everything worth seeing. It is a plan that
holds up when it rains, when the museum is shut, and when the morning runs
forty minutes late.

## The day entry

Every day carries the same shape. See
[day-structure.md](references/day-structure.md) for the full form.

- **One anchor.** The thing the day is built around, named specifically — a
  particular museum, a particular walk, a booked lunch. One, not three.
- **One backup.** What happens instead if the anchor falls through: weather,
  closure, illness, or simply not feeling like it. Indoor if the anchor was
  outdoor.
- **Loose surroundings.** What else is nearby and worth doing, without times
  attached.
- **Meals**, where they need booking or where the area is thin.
- **Where you sleep**, when it changes.

Times only where something is actually timed. A day plan full of invented
times is a schedule the traveller will fall behind by mid-morning and then
ignore entirely.

## Pacing

- **One anchor per day.** Two is a busy day and should be declared as one.
  Three is a list, not a plan.
- **A rest slot on arrival day and after every long transfer.** Not optional,
  and not "see how you feel" — write it in, and it will be taken.
- **A genuinely empty half-day every three or four days.** The first thing
  people cut and the first thing they miss.
- **Match the pace to the party.** Small children and anyone with mobility
  constraints roughly halve what a day holds.

## Travel time, honestly

Take the published journey time and add the real parts: getting to the station,
buying or collecting tickets, the walk at the far end, and a margin.

- City crossings take longer than the map says. Budget the map time plus half.
- A "day trip" that is two hours each way is a five-hour commitment before
  anything happens at the destination.
- Intercity travel days are travel days. Do not also plan an anchor.
- Check the last return service before planning an evening somewhere else.

## Closed days and opening hours

The most common failure in a plausible-looking itinerary.

- **Museums close on Mondays** across much of Europe; some close Tuesdays.
- **Sunday closes shops and some restaurants** in Germany, Switzerland and
  Austria, and much of the retail in France.
- **Lunch closures** are real in Italy, Spain and smaller French towns.
- **Seasonal hours** differ sharply from summer hours, and some attractions
  shut entirely out of season.
- **Last admission** is routinely an hour before closing.

Check the specific opening hours for anything the day is built on, and check
them for the actual date, not in general. Where the **destination-calendar**
skill found a holiday, re-check: holiday hours differ from both weekday and
weekend hours.

## The reservation tracker

The part with a deadline is the part that matters. See
[lead-times.md](references/lead-times.md).

- **The deadline is a computed date**, not "book early". Work back from the
  release window and put a real date in the column.
- **Sort by deadline**, not by trip order. The thing to book first is rarely
  the thing that happens first.
- **Say what happens if it is missed** — a longer queue, or the day falls apart.
- **Record the state**: not booked, booked, or confirmed with a reference.

## Packing and preparation

Driven by what the itinerary actually contains, never generic. A day on a
glacier, a dinner with a dress code and a long-haul flight each add specific
items; a beach week adds almost nothing worth listing.

Cover documents and their copies, adapters for the actual plug type, medication
with enough margin for a delayed return, and anything the destination is
genuinely hard to buy in.

## Rules

- **Never invent an opening hour, a price or a journey time.** Retrieve it or
  mark it as needing a check.
- **Name things specifically.** "A good local restaurant" is not a plan.
- **Say why an anchor is there**, in a line. It lets the traveller swap it for
  something they would prefer.
- **Leave the gaps in deliberately** and say they are deliberate, so they do not
  read as unfinished work.
- **Re-check before departure.** Opening hours and services move. Put the
  re-check list in the deliverable.
